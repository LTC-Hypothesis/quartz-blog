# $L^p$-theory for Parabolic Eqs. ($1<p<\infty$)

Use the notation of [[10. Schauder Theory for Parabolic Equations]]:
$$
\mathscr Lu=u_t-a_{ij}u_{x_ix_j}+b_iu_{x_i}+cu.
$$
Assume uniform parabolicity, $a_{ij}\in C(\overline{Q_T})$, $b_i,c\in L^\infty(Q_T)$, and $\partial\Omega\in C^2$ for global and boundary estimates.

> [!def] Strong solution
> A strong sol of IBVP lies in
> $$
> u\in W_p^{2,1}(Q_T),\qquad u|_{S_T}=0,
> $$
> satisfies $\mathscr Lu=f$ a.e., and $u(\cdot,0)=\varphi$ in the time-trace sense. The manuscript encodes zero lateral data by also requiring $u\in\dot W_p^{1,1}(Q_T)$.

## Global existence and initial trace

> [!note] 整理注：the initial-data space on p. 44
> The manuscript writes $\varphi\in W^{1,p}(\Omega)$ for every $p\ne3/2$. For general $p$, the time trace of $W_p^{2,1}$ is $W^{2-2/p,p}$ (the corresponding Besov trace space $B_{p,p}^{2-2/p}$); it reduces to $H^1$ when $p=2$. For $p>2$, $W^{1,p}$ alone is insufficient for the claimed global $W_p^{2,1}$ regularity. The theorem below uses the corrected trace space. For $1<p<2$, the manuscript's $W^{1,p}$ assumption is a stronger sufficient initial regularity.

> [!thm]
> Let $1<p<\infty$, $p\ne3/2$. For $f\in L^p(Q_T)$ and
> $$\varphi\in W^{2-2/p,p}(\Omega),$$
> impose $\varphi|_{\partial\Omega}=0$ when $p>3/2$. Then IBVP has a unique strong sol and
> $$
> \|u\|_{W_p^{2,1}(Q_T)}\le C(\|f\|_{L^p(Q_T)}+\|\varphi\|_{W^{2-2/p,p}(\Omega)}).
> $$

The threshold is obtained from $2-2/p>1/p$, i.e. $p>3/2$, when a boundary trace of the initial datum is defined in this scale. The critical case $p=3/2$ is excluded in the manuscript and is not treated here.

## Interior estimates

> [!thm]
> If $u\in W_p^{2,1}(Q_T)$ solves $\mathscr Lu=f\in L^p(Q_T)$ a.e., then for a subregion $Q'$ separated from the parabolic boundary,
> $$
> \|u\|_{W_p^{2,1}(Q')}\le C(\|f\|_{L^p(Q_T)}+\|u\|_{L^p(Q_T)}).
> $$

## Boundary estimates

> [!thm]
> If $u=0$ on $S_T$ and $\partial\Omega\in C^2$, then for every $0<\varepsilon<T$,
> $$
> \|u\|_{W_p^{2,1}(\Omega\times(\varepsilon,T))}\le C_\varepsilon(\|f\|_{L^p(Q_T)}+\|u\|_{L^p(Q_T)}).
> $$

The manuscript states these estimates without proof. Compare the energy method in [[8. L2-theory for parabolic equations]] and the nonlinear application in [[7. Existence and Uniqueness of nonlinear heat equations]].
