我们考虑的系统为
$$
\begin{cases}
\dot{y}=f(t,y(t),u(t)),a.e. \ t\in[0,T]\\
y(0)=y_0
\end{cases}\tag{1}
$$
指标泛函为
$$
J(u(\cdot))=\int_{0}^{T}f^0(t,y(t),u(t)){d}t
$$

>[!def] 假设(C1)-(C3)
>- (C1) $U\subseteq\mathbb{R}^m,T>0$
>- (C2) $f:[0,T]\times\mathbb{R}^n\times U\to\mathbb{R}^n,f^0:[0,T]\times\mathbb{R}^n\times U\to\mathbb{R}$都是可测的，且存在常数$L>0$和连续模$\omega:[0,+\infty)\to[0,+\infty)$使得对$\varphi=f,f^0$成立
>  $$
>  \begin{cases}
|\varphi(t,y,u)-\varphi(t,\hat{y},\hat{u})|\le L|y-\hat{y}|+\omega(|u-\hat{u}|)\\
|\varphi(t,0,u)|\le L
\end{cases},\forall t\in[0,T],y,\hat{y}\in\mathbb{R}^n,u,\hat{u}\in U
>  $$
>- (C3) $f,f^0$关于$y$是$C^1$的，且存在连续模$\omega:[0,+\infty)\to[0,+\infty)$使得对$\varphi=f,f^0$成立
>   $$
>  |\varphi_y(t,y,u)-\varphi_y(t,\hat{y},\hat{u})|\le\omega(|y-\hat{y}|+|u-\hat{u}|)
>  $$

>[!def] 问题（C）
>寻找最优控制$\bar{u}(\cdot)\in\mathscr{U}[0,T]$使得
>$$
>J(\bar{u}(\cdot))=\inf_{u(\cdot)\in\mathscr{U}[0,T]}J(u(\cdot))
>$$

>[!thm] $\mathbb{R}^m$上的最大值原理
>设$U=\mathbb{R}^m,f^0,f$关于$y$和$u$有连续一阶偏导数，$f_y,f^0_y,f_u,f^0_u$一致有界。若$(\bar{y}(\cdot),\bar{u}(\cdot))$是问题（C）的一个最优对，则存在函数$\bar{\psi}(\cdot):[0,T]\to\mathbb{R}^n$满足
>$$
>\begin{cases}
\dot{\bar{\psi}}=-f_y(t,\bar{y}(t),\bar{u}(t))\bar{\psi}(t)+f^0_y(t,\bar{y}(t),\bar{u}(t))\\
\bar{\psi}(T)=0
\end{cases}\tag{2}
>$$
>使得
>$$
>f_u(t,\bar{y}(t),\bar{u}(t))\bar{\psi}(t)=f^0_u(t,\bar{y}(t),\bar{u}(t))\tag{3}
>$$
>在$[0,T]$上几乎处处成立。

**Proof**
证明的想法来自[[古典变分法]]。
任取$u(\cdot)\in L^\infty(0,T;\mathbb{R}^m)\subset\mathscr{U}[0,T]$，则对任何$\alpha\in\mathbb{R}$，记$u^\alpha\triangleq \bar{u}(\cdot)+\alpha u(\cdot)\in\mathscr{U}[0,T]$。古典变分告诉我们，$J(u^\alpha(\cdot))$在$\alpha=0$取到最小值，只需要计算
$$
\frac{d}{d\alpha}J(u^\alpha(\cdot))\mid_{\alpha=0}=0
$$
对$\alpha$，记$y^\alpha(\cdot)=y(\cdot;u^\alpha(\cdot))$。考虑
$$
\begin{aligned}
0\le &\frac{J(u^\alpha(\cdot))-J(\bar{u}(\cdot))}{\alpha}=\int_{0}^{T}\frac{f^0(t,y^\alpha(t),u^\alpha(t))-f^0(t,\bar{y}(t),\bar{u}(t))}{\alpha}{d}t\\
=&\int_{0}^{T}\int_{0}^{1}\left\langle f^0_y(t,\bar{y}+\theta(y^\alpha-\bar{y}),\bar{u}+\theta\alpha u),\frac{y^\alpha-\bar{y}}{\alpha}\right\rangle{d}\theta{d}t\qquad\text{(中值定理)}\\
&+\int_{0}^{T}\int_{0}^{1}\left\langle f^0_u(t,\bar{y}+\theta(y^\alpha-\bar{y}),\bar{u}+\theta\alpha u),u\right\rangle{d}\theta{d}t\\
:=&\int_{0}^{T}\int_{0}^{1}\left\langle f^0_y(t,\bar{y}+\theta(y^\alpha-\bar{y}),\bar{u}+\theta\alpha u),Y^\alpha\right\rangle{d}\theta{d}t\\
&+\int_{0}^{T}\int_{0}^{1}\left\langle f^0_u(t,\bar{y}+\theta(y^\alpha-\bar{y}),\bar{u}+\theta\alpha u),u\right\rangle{d}\theta{d}t
\end{aligned}
$$
其中$Y^\alpha$满足
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{dY^\alpha(t)}{dt}=&\frac{1}{\alpha}(f(t,y^\alpha,u^\alpha)-f(t,\bar{y},\bar{u}))\\
=&\int_{0}^{1}f_y(t,\bar{y}+\theta(y^\alpha-\bar{y}),\bar{u}+\theta\alpha u)^\top{d}\theta\cdot Y^\alpha+\int_{0}^{1}f_u(t,\bar{y}+\theta(y^\alpha-\bar{y}),\bar{u}+\theta\alpha u)^\top u{d}\theta
\end{aligned}
$$
设$f^0_y,f^0_u,f_y,f_u$的一致界为$M$，则有
$$
\left|Y^\alpha(t)\right|\le M\int_{0}^{t}Y^\alpha(s){d}s+M\int_{0}^{T}u(\tau){d}\tau
$$
由[[Strong solutions#^5fc0f3|Gronwall不等式]]，
$$
|Y^\alpha(t)|\le Me^{MT}\int_{0}^{T}u(\tau){d}\tau 
$$
于是$Y^\alpha(t)$一致有界，因此有
$$
\lim_{\alpha\to0}y^\alpha(t)=\lim_{\alpha\to0}(\bar{y}(t)+\alpha Y^\alpha(t))=\bar{y}(t)
$$
我们需要ODE的解关于参数的连续性。
![[Pasted image 20260706111551.png]]
于是有
$$
\|Y^\alpha(\cdot)-Y(\cdot)\|_{C([0,T])}\to0 \ \text{ as }\alpha\to0
$$
其中$Y(\cdot)$为以下方程的解
$$
\begin{cases}
\frac{dY(t)}{dt}=f_y(t,\bar{y},\bar{u})^\top Y(t)+f_u(t,\bar{y},\bar{u})^\top u\\
Y(0)=0
\end{cases}
$$
假设$\bar{\psi}(t)$由方程（2）确定，于是有
$$
\begin{aligned}
0\le &\int_{0}^{T}f_y^0(t,\bar{y},\bar{u})^\top Y(t){d}t+\int_{0}^{T}f^0_u(t,\bar{y},\bar{u})^\top u(t){d}t\\
=&\int_{0}^{T}\left\langle\frac{d\bar{\psi}(t)}{dt},Y(t)\right\rangle+\left\langle f_y(t,\bar{y},\bar{u})\bar{\psi}(t),Y(t)\right\rangle{d}t+\int_{0}^{T}\left\langle f_u^0(t,\bar{y},\bar{u}),u(t)\right\rangle{d}t\\

=&-\int_{0}^{T}\left\langle\bar{\psi},\frac{d}{dt}Y(t)\right\rangle+\left\langle f_y(t,\bar{y},\bar{u})\bar{\psi}(t),Y(t)\right\rangle{d}t+\int_{0}^{T}\left\langle f_u^0(t,\bar{y},\bar{u}),u(t)\right\rangle{d}t\\
=&-\int_{0}^{T}\left\langle f_u(t,\bar{y},\bar{u})\bar{\psi}, u(t)\right\rangle{d}t+\int_{0}^{T}\left\langle f_u^0(t,\bar{y},\bar{u}),u(t)\right\rangle{d}t\\
\end{aligned}
$$
由$u(\cdot)$的任意性，即可得到最大值条件$(3)$。
**QED**

>[!thm] 一般控制集上的最大值原理
>[[#^1a105e|假设(C1)-(C3)]]成立，$(\bar{y}(\cdot),\bar{u}(\cdot))$为问题（C）的一个最优对，则存在$\bar{\psi}(\cdot):[0,T]\to\mathbb{R}^n$满足
>$$
>\begin{cases}
\dot{\bar{\psi}}(t)=-f_y(t,\bar{y}(t),\bar{u}(t))\bar{\psi}(t)+f^0_y(t,\bar{y}(t),\bar{u}(t))\\
\bar{\psi}(T)=0
\end{cases}\tag{4}
>$$
>以及以下最大值条件在$[0,T]$上几乎处处成立，
>$$
>\begin{aligned}
>&\left\langle\bar{\psi}(t),f(t,\bar{y}(t),\bar{u}(t))\right\rangle-f^0(t,\bar{y}(t),\bar{u}(t))\\
>=&\max_{u(\cdot)\in U}\left\langle\bar{\psi}(t),f(t,\bar{y}(t),u)\right\rangle-f^0(t,\bar{y}(t),u)
>\end{aligned}\tag{5}
>$$

>[!warning] Hamilton系统
>我们记**Hamilton函数**为
>$$
>H(t,y,u,\psi)\triangleq\left\langle\psi,f(t,y,u)\right\rangle-f^0(t,y,u)\tag{6}
>$$
>于是将系统改写为
>$$
>\begin{cases}
\frac{d\bar{y}(t)}{dt}=\frac{\partial H}{\partial\psi}(t,\bar{y},\bar{u},\bar{\psi})\\
\frac{d\bar{\psi}(t)}{dt}=-\frac{\partial H}{\partial y}(t,\bar{y},\bar{u},\bar{\psi})
\end{cases}\tag{7}
>$$
>我们将$(7)$称为**Hamilton系统**，方程（4）称为伴随方程。

**Proof**
证明的想法是针状变分。
**QED**
