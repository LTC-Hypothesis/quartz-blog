> [!def] 假设(L2)
> $U\subseteq\mathbb{R}^m$为非空紧集，$M:[0,+\infty)\to 2^{\mathbb{R}^n}$关于Hausdorff度量$\rho$连续，且对每个$t\in[0,\infty)$, $M(t)$是非空凸集。

^4d79f5

由于$U$是紧集，因此$\mathscr{U}^p[0,\infty)$都相同简记为$\mathscr{U}[0,\infty)$。对于$t\ge t_0$，能达集$\mathscr{R}(t)$是[[能达集#^837992|凸紧集]]。
> [!def] 能控性假设
> 我们假设
> $$
> \bigcup_{t\ge t_0}\{M(t)\cap \mathscr{R}(t;t_0,y_0)\}\ne\emptyset\tag{1}
> $$
> 这表示从$(t_0,y_0)$到目标$M(\cdot)$都是能控的。

^bd2b7f

> [!def] 指标泛函
> 定义
> $$
> J(u(\cdot))=\inf\{t\ge t_0:y(t;t_0,y_0,u(\cdot))\in M(\cdot)\}\tag{2}
> $$
> $J(u(\cdot))$为状态轨线$y(\cdot;t_0,y_0,u(\cdot))$到目标$M(\cdot)$的**首中时**。

> [!question] 时间最优控制问题(TC)
> 给定$(t_0,y_0)\in[0,\infty)\times\mathbb{R}^n$，假设能控性条件成立，寻找$\bar{u}(\cdot)\in\mathscr{U}[0,\infty)$ s.t. 
> $$
> J(\bar{u}(\cdot);t_0,y_0)=\inf_{u(\cdot)\in\mathscr{U}[t_0,\infty)}J(u(\cdot);t_0,y_0)\tag{TC}
> $$
> 最优时间为$\bar{t}=J(\bar{u}(\cdot);t_0,y_0)$

**Proof**

> [!thm] 最优时间的刻画
> 假设[[能控性#^893f33|(L1)]], [[#^4d79f5|(L2)]]和[[#^bd2b7f|能控性假设]]成立，$y_0\notin M(0)$，$\bar{t}$为[[#^06ca5a|问题(TC)]]的最优时间，则$\bar{t}$满足
> $$
> [\partial M(\bar{t})]\cap[\partial\mathscr{R}(\bar{t})]=M(\bar{t})\cap\mathscr{R}(\bar{t})\ne\emptyset\tag{5}
> $$
> $M(\cdot)=\left\{z(\cdot)\right\}$是单值函数，$(5)$式变为$z(\bar{t})\in\partial\mathscr{R}(\bar{t})$.

**Proof**
由$(4)$式已经给出$M(\bar{t})\cap\mathscr{R}(\bar{t})\ne\emptyset$且由于$M(\cdot),\mathscr{R}(\cdot)$都是闭集，因此$[\partial M(\bar{t})]\cap[\partial\mathscr{R}(\bar{t})]\subseteq M(\bar{t})\cap\mathscr{R}(\bar{t})$。若另一个包含方向不成立，则存在$\bar{z}$，有以下情况
$$
\bar{z}\in M(\bar{t})\cap\mathring{\mathscr{R}}(\bar{t})\text{ or }\bar{z}\in \mathring{M}(\bar{t})\cap\mathscr{R}(\bar{t})
$$
不妨假设为第一种情况。**Claim：$\exists\delta>0,\tilde{t}\in(0,\bar{t})$ s.t. $M(\tilde{t})\cap B_\delta(\bar{z})\ne\emptyset,B_\delta(\bar{z})\subseteq\mathscr{R}(\tilde{t})$**。
若断言不成立，由于$\mathring{\mathscr{R}}(\bar{t})$为开集，因此存在$\delta>0$使得$B_{2\delta}(\bar{z})\subseteq\mathscr{R}(\bar{t})$且$M(\cdot),\mathscr{R}(\cdot)$关于Hausdorff度量连续，因此可以选取$\tilde{t}\in(0,\bar{t})$使得
$$
\left.\begin{matrix}
\rho(M(\tilde{t}),M(\bar{t}))<\frac{\delta}{2}\\
\rho(\mathscr{R}(\tilde{t}),\mathscr{R}(\bar{t}))<\frac{\delta}{2}
\end{matrix}\right\}\Longrightarrow d(\bar{z},M(\tilde{t}))\le2\rho(M(\tilde{t}),M(\bar{t}))<\delta
$$
因此$\exists\tilde{z}\in M(\tilde{t})$使得$|\bar{z}-\tilde{z}|<\delta$。由于我们假设断言$B_\delta(\bar{z})\subseteq\mathscr{R}(\tilde{t})$不成立，因此存在$\xi\in B_\delta(\bar{z})$但是$\xi\notin\mathscr{R}(\tilde{t})$。由于$\mathscr{R}(\tilde{t})$是凸闭集，我们需要以下引理
![[Pasted image 20260618162308.png]]
根据引理可知，存在$\tilde{r}\in\mathscr{R}(\tilde{t})$使得成立
$$
\begin{cases}
|\xi-\tilde{r}|=d(\xi,\mathscr{R}(\tilde{t}))\\
\langle\xi-\tilde{r},r-\tilde{r}\rangle,\forall r\in\mathscr{R}(\tilde{t})
\end{cases}\tag{6}
$$
取$z=\xi+\delta\frac{\xi-\tilde{r}}{|\xi-\tilde{r}|}\in\mathscr{R}(\tilde{t})$，则
$$
\begin{align}
|z-\bar{z}|\le |z-\xi|+|\xi-\bar{z}|<\delta+\delta=2\delta\Longrightarrow z\in B_{2\delta}(\bar{z})
\end{align}
$$
且
$$
\begin{cases}
|z-\tilde{r}|=|\xi-\tilde{r}|\left(1+\frac{\delta}{|\xi-\tilde{r}|}\right)>\delta\\
\langle z-\tilde{r},r-\tilde{r}\rangle=\left(1+\frac{\delta}{|\xi-\tilde{r}|}\right)\langle\xi-\tilde{r},r-\tilde{r}\rangle\le0
\end{cases}\Longrightarrow d(z,\mathscr{R}(\tilde{t}))>\delta
$$
但是$z\in B_{2\delta}(\bar{z})\subseteq\mathscr{R}(\bar{t})$，于是有
$$
d(z,\mathscr{R}(\tilde{t}))\le 2\rho(\mathscr{R}(\tilde{t}),\mathscr{R}(\bar{t}))<\delta\Longrightarrow\Longleftarrow\text{ with }d(z,\mathscr{R}(\tilde{t}))>\delta
$$
所以断言成立。因此$M(\tilde{t})\cap\mathscr{R}(\tilde{t})\ne\emptyset$且$\tilde{t}<\bar{t}$与$\bar{t}$为最优时间矛盾，因此最开始的假设不对。最终$[\partial M(\bar{t})]\cap[\partial\mathscr{R}(\bar{t})]=M(\bar{t})\cap\mathscr{R}(\bar{t})$。
**QED**
> [!thm] 最优控制的最大值条件
> 假设[[能控性#^893f33|(L1)]], [[#^4d79f5|(L2)]]和[[#^bd2b7f|能控性假设]]成立，$y_0\notin M(0)$，$\bar{t}$为[[#^06ca5a|问题(TC)]]的最优时间，则$\bar{t}$为下面函数在$[0,\infty)$的最小零点：
> $$
> F(t)=\inf_{|\lambda|=1}\left\{\max_{z\in M(t)}\langle\lambda,\Phi(t,0)y_0-z\rangle+\int_{0}^{t}\max_{u\in U}\langle\lambda,\Phi(t,s)B(s)u(s)\rangle{d}s\right\}\tag{7}
> $$
> 若对$|\lambda_0|=1$满足
> $$
> \left\{\max_{z\in M(\bar{t})}\langle\lambda_0,\Phi(\bar{t},0)y_0-z\rangle+\int_{0}^{\bar{t}}\max_{u\in U}\langle\lambda_0,\Phi(\bar{t},s)B(s)u(s)\rangle{d}s\right\}=0\tag{8}
> $$
> 则最优控制$\bar{u}(\cdot)$满足以下最大值条件：
> $$
> \max_{u\in U}\langle\lambda_0,\Phi(\bar{t},s)B(s)u(s)\rangle=\langle\lambda_0,\Phi(\bar{t},s)B(s)\bar{u}(s)\rangle,a.e. \ s\in[0,\overline{t}]\tag{9}
> $$
> $\bar{y}:=y(\bar{t};\bar{u}(\cdot))$横截条件：
> $$
> \langle\lambda_0,z-\bar{y}\rangle\ge0,\forall z\in M(\bar{t})\tag{10}
> $$

**Proof**
我们需要[[能达集#^4f0efe|Filippov引理]]。对固定的$a.e. \ s\in[0,t]$，记
$$
y(t)=\max_{u\in U}\langle\lambda,\Phi(t,s)B(s)u\rangle,f(t,U)=\langle\lambda,\Phi(t,s)B(s)U\rangle
$$
[[能达集#^4f0efe|Filippov引理]]告诉我们，存在$\hat{u}(\cdot)\in\mathscr{U}[0,\infty)$使得
$$
y(t)=f(t,\hat{u}(t))\Longleftrightarrow\max_{u\in U}\langle\lambda,\Phi(t,s)B(s)u(s)\rangle=\langle\lambda,\Phi(t,s)B(s)\hat{u}(s)\rangle,a.e. \ s\in[0,t]
$$
两边对$s$积分，
$$
\begin{align}
\int_{0}^{t}\max_{u\in U}\langle\lambda,\Phi(t,s)B(s)u(s)\rangle{d}s&=\int_{0}^{t}\langle\lambda,\Phi(t,s)B(s)\hat{u}(s)\rangle{d}s\\
&=\max_{u\in\mathscr{U}[0,t]}\int_{0}^{t}\langle\lambda,\Phi(t,s)B(s)u(s)\rangle{d}s
\end{align}
$$
于是
$$
\begin{align}
F(t)&=\inf_{|\lambda|=1}\left\{\max_{z\in M(t)}\langle\lambda,\Phi(t,0)y_0-z\rangle+\int_{0}^{t}\max_{u\in U}\langle\lambda,\Phi(t,s)B(s)u(s)\rangle{d}s\right\}\\
&=\inf_{|\lambda|=1}\left\{\max_{z\in M(t)}\langle\lambda,\Phi(t,0)y_0-z\rangle+\max_{u\in\mathscr{U}[0,t]}\int_{0}^{t}\langle\lambda,\Phi(t,s)B(s)u(s)\rangle{d}s\right\}\\
&=\inf_{|\lambda|=1}\left\{\max_{z\in M(t),u\in\mathscr{U}[0,t]}\left\langle\lambda,\textcolor{blue}{\Phi(t,0)y_0+\int_{0}^{t}\Phi(t,s)B(s)u(s){d}s}-z\right\rangle\right\}\\
&=\inf_{|\lambda|=1}\left\{\max_{z\in M(t),y\in\mathscr{R}(t)}\left\langle\lambda,y-z\right\rangle\right\}\\
&=\inf_{|\lambda|=1}\left\{\max_{y\in\mathscr{R}(t)- M(t)}\left\langle\lambda,y\right\rangle\right\}
\end{align}
$$
若$0\notin\mathscr{R}(t)-M(t)\Longrightarrow\mathscr{R}(t)\cap M(t)=\emptyset$，我们需要下面的凸集分离定理：
![[Pasted image 20260620225324.png]]
因此存在$c<0,\lambda\in\mathbb{R}^n,|\lambda|=1$使得
$$
0>c\ge\langle\lambda,y\rangle,y\in\mathscr{R}(t)-M(t)\Longrightarrow F(t)\le c<0
$$
若$0\in\mathscr{R}(t)-M(t)$，对任何$\lambda\in\mathbb{R}^n,|\lambda|=1$, 
$$
\max_{y\in\mathscr{R}(t)-M(t)}\langle\lambda,y\rangle\ge0\Longrightarrow F(t)\ge0
$$
由[[#^096506|最优时间的刻画]]可知，$\mathscr{R}(t)\cap M(t)\ne\emptyset\Longrightarrow0\in\mathscr{R}(t)-M(t)$，因此有
$$
F(t)<0,\forall t\in[0,\bar{t});F(t)\ge0,\forall t\in[\bar{t},\infty)\Longrightarrow F(\bar{t})=0
$$
现在取$\lambda_0\in\mathbb{R},|\lambda_0|=1$使得$(8)$式成立，记最优控制为$\bar{u}(\cdot)$，则此时最优状态为
$$
\bar{y}=\bar{y}(\bar{t};y_0,u(\cdot))=\Phi(\bar{t},0)y_0+\int_{0}^{\bar{t}}\Phi(\bar{t},s)B(s)\bar{u}(s){d}s\in\mathscr{R}(\bar{t})\cap M(\bar{t})
$$
于是有
$$
\begin{align}
0&=\max_{z\in M(\bar{t})}\langle\lambda,\Phi(\bar{t},0)y_0-z\rangle+\int_{0}^{\bar{t}}\max_{u\in U}\langle\lambda,\Phi(\bar{t},s)B(s)u(s)\rangle{d}s\\
&\ge\langle\lambda,\Phi(\bar{t},0)y_0-\bar{y}\rangle+\int_{0}^{\bar{t}}\langle\lambda,\Phi(\bar{t},s)B(s)\bar{u}(s)\rangle{d}s=0
\end{align}
$$
这说明
$$
\begin{align}
&\max_{z\in M(\bar{t})}\langle\lambda,\Phi(\bar{t},0)y_0-z\rangle=\langle\lambda,\Phi(\bar{t},0)y_0-\bar{y}
\rangle\\
\text{ and }&\int_{0}^{\bar{t}}\max_{u\in U}\langle\lambda,\Phi(\bar{t},s)B(s)u(s)\rangle-\langle\lambda,\Phi(\bar{t},s)B(s)\bar{u}(s)\rangle{d}s=0
\end{align}
$$
由被积函数的非负性，得到最大值条件$(9)$和横截条件$(10)$。
**QED**

> [!thm] Bang-Bang原理
> 假设[[#^4d79f5|(L2)]]和[[#^bd2b7f|能控性假设]]成立，则存在最优控制$\bar{u}(\cdot)\in\mathscr{U}[0,\bar{t}]$使得$\bar{u}(t)\in\partial U,a.e. \ t\in[0,\bar{t}]$

**Proof**
我们记
$$
\mathscr{W}[0,\infty)=\left\{w(\cdot)\in L^\infty_{loc}(\mathbb{R}^m):w(t)\in\partial U\right\}
$$
$$
\mathscr{V}[0,\infty)=\left\{v(\cdot)\in L^\infty_{loc}(\mathbb{R}^m):v(t)\in\text{co}(\partial U)\right\}
$$
注意到
$$
\partial U\subseteq U\subseteq\text{co}(U)=\text{co}(\partial U)
$$
从而对应控制的能达集有以下包含关系
$$
\left\{y(t;w(\cdot)):w(\cdot)\in\mathscr{W}[0,\infty)\right\}\subseteq\mathscr{R}(t)\subseteq\left\{y(t;v(\cdot)):v(\cdot)\in\mathscr{V}[0,\infty)\right\}
$$
实际上根据[[能达集#^837992|能达集凸紧性的证明]]，成立
$$
\left\{y(t;w(\cdot)):w(\cdot)\in\mathscr{W}[0,\infty)\right\}=\left\{y(t;v(\cdot)):v(\cdot)\in\mathscr{V}[0,\infty)\right\}=\mathscr{R}(t),\forall t\ge0
$$
这样对于最优控制$\bar{u}(\cdot)\in\mathscr{U}[0,\bar{t}]$, 存在$w(\cdot)\in\mathscr{W}[0,\bar{t}]$使得$y(\bar{t};\bar{u}(\cdot))=y(\bar{t};\bar{w}(\cdot))$
**QED**

>[!warning] $\text{co}(U)=\text{co}(\partial U)$
>由于$U$为非空紧集，因此$\partial U\subseteq U\Longrightarrow\text{co}(\partial U)\subseteq\text{co}(U)$。另一方面，假设反方向不成立，则存在$x\in\text{co}(U)\setminus\text{co}(\partial U)$。由于$\partial U$也是闭集，从而是紧集且$\text{co}(\partial U)$为凸紧集。对于$\{x\}$和$\text{co}(\partial U)$，可以使用Hahn-Banach定理进行分离，因此存在$\alpha\in\mathbb{R}$和非零线性泛函$f:\mathbb{R}^n\to\mathbb{R}$使得
>$$
>f(x)>\alpha>f(y),\forall y\in\text{co}(\partial U)
>$$
>由于$x\in\text{co}(U)$，因此可以写成$U$中的点的凸组合，即
>$$

>$$

>$$
>f(u)\ge f(x)>\alpha>\beta\ge\max_{z\in\partial U}f(z)
